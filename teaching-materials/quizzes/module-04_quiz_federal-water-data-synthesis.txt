-   SWORD reach IDs encode region and reach type, not gage numbers, and the number of observations says nothing about where the gage is.

c)  Reach IDs encode the gage number, so read the gage from the reach ID instead.
d)  Choose the reach with the most overpasses, since it is the best observed one.
